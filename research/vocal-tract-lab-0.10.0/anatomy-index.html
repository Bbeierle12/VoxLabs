What anatomy is represented?

The airway lumen comes from the frozen MRI atlas. Its 32 cross-sections change area around a fixed centerline using four bounded log-area modes. The glottis is an inlet boundary, not a vocal-fold simulation. Tongue, palate, lips, teeth, velum, hyoid, and individual muscles have no independently segmented 3D tissues in this model.

All interactive modes use this same airway and acoustic solver. F0 sets harmonic spacing; it cannot determine a unique tongue pose. The standing-mode display illustrates harmonics on a normalized interval; it is not a pressure field computed inside the irregular airway.

Numerical state / reproducibility

One native state contains shape coordinates, pitch, mixer, geometry, and response. Settings save locally. Playback and microphone capture require an explicit tap after returning to the app.

Read the labels carefully

Head combines upper lip, hard palate, skull/brain, posterior pharyngeal wall and neck. Jaw combines lower lip and adjacent soft tissues, not isolated bone. Tongue includes epiglottis and hyoid. Tooth space is not teeth.

The source provides expert labels; our project has not independently accepted these as a 3D anatomical baseline.

How much data do we have?

Enough for this constrained research prototype; insufficient for a validated singing anatomy model. The five 3D subjects each have different vowel conditions, so identity and articulation are confounded. The separate 392-frame MRI label viewer adds 2D reference evidence, not additional 3D subjects.

The next anatomical improvement needs licensed metric volumes with repeated vowels per person, matching tongue/lip/jaw/velum labels, paired acoustics, and independent expert review. Voice-part names and acoustic recordings alone cannot supply missing tissue geometry.

One model throughout

Vowel targets fit the same bounded MRI model used by live analysis. The airway sections drive response, sound, and waveform. Targets and achieved resonances appear separately; a poor fit is never silently relabeled as the requested vowel.

The frozen atlas and its failed acceptance gates remain unchanged. No new subject or expert approval was added in this update.
